## OPENING STATE

The rider stands barefoot at the outer lip of a glass observation deck near the top of the Camlica Tower in Istanbul, on a clear warm night. Her two bare feet and short black glossy wet-look shorts fill the bottom third of the frame, her skin lit warm amber from below. Beneath the glass, straight down through it, the city is already visible: dense hills of low buildings glowing with warm street light, and beyond them the Bosphorus lying black and wide, crossed by a lit suspension bridge whose cables burn warm orange. ## BEATS

[0.0-2.0] The rider stands motionless at the edge and the camera on her chest holds the drop dead centre. Her bare toes press against the warm glass. The whole of Istanbul lies directly beneath them through the floor, an unbroken field of amber street light running down the hills to the black water. The transparent slide mouth waits ahead of her, its amber strips the same colour as the city, so nothing in the frame reads as artificial light. The stillness is the fear.

[2.0-4.0] She edges forward toward the mouth in short unsteady steps. It grows continuously and never jumps in size: first a small amber outline, then a pair of transparent upswept wings taller than her knees, then a wide open throat. Behind it the city keeps its exact place in the frame and slides down slightly as she closes on the edge. Warm light from the strips runs up her bare shins. Her legs shake visibly.

[4.0-6.0] She sits and drops into the throat of the slide. The deck leaves the frame entirely and does not come back. The two amber strips snap into hard converging perspective, running away from her hips to a vanishing point far below. Through the transparent floor between her legs there is now nothing but open air and the lit hills of Istanbul directly under her. The first steep pitch takes her and the horizon is thrown up and out of the top of the frame.

[6.0-8.0] The slide bottoms out and a sheet of water bursts up around her hips and across the lens. Spray floods the frame for a moment, lit warm gold from beneath by the edge strips, then clears in streaks. Her bare legs are soaked and shining. The camera pumps its exposure as the frame swings from dark sky to bright water and back. Speed becomes obvious: the two strips are now streaming past the frame edges.

[8.0-10.0] The slide banks hard right and the entire city rotates around her legs while her legs stay in the same place in the frame. The amber hills tilt up the left side of the frame and the black Bosphorus fills the right. Ahead and below, already visible against the haze, the lit suspension bridge stretches across the water, growing steadily as she closes on it.

[10.0-11.5] She holds that bank. The city rolls all the way around the frame, once, continuously, never cutting and never jumping. Water flies outward along the curve. Coming out of it the horizon settles far lower than before and the bridge is no longer ahead but beneath her, its warm orange cables passing directly under the transparent floor.

[11.5-13.0] The slide drops into a corridor between two dark hillside apartment blocks. Lit windows stream past on both sides of the frame, close and fast, throwing warm rectangles across the wet acrylic and across her bare legs. Straight ahead and below, already visible and growing, a rectangular rooftop pool glows warm gold between the buildings. The final chute aims directly at it.

[13.0-15.0] She hits the water. The frame goes to churning white for a beat, then punches through into the pool: warm, gold-lit, bubble-filled, quiet under the surface. She rises and breaks through. The lens clears in streaks and the last thing in the frame is her two bare feet floating in glowing amber water with the dark hillside buildings standing around the pool and the lights of Istanbul still burning beyond them.
