# MASTER BLOCK

degismez. Degistirirsen butun rotalar degisir.

Bolum sirasi uretimde sabittir:
FORMAT, INDEPENDENCE NOTE, CAMERA, RIDER, SLIDE, MOTION AND GRAVITY, WORLD, AUDIO,
OPENING STATE, TIMELINE, VOICE, END STATE, NEGATIVE.

## FORMAT

Create a single continuous <<DURATION>>-second vertical 9:16 video at 1080x1920, 30 frames per second. Photorealistic live-action action-camera footage, not animation, not a render, not a game. Record the WHOLE video as ONE SINGLE UNBROKEN CONTINUOUS TAKE from the first frame to the last. There is no cut, no transition and no seam anywhere. The footage must feel like a real waterproof action camera recording one real ride in real time.

## INDEPENDENCE NOTE

This prompt is the complete video. It is not a fragment, not a shot in a sequence, and it does not continue from or lead into any other clip. The entire ride, from standing at the top to surfacing in the pool, happens inside this one generation. Everything required to reconstruct the camera, the rider's body, the slide, the city, the motion, the sound and the exact opening condition is explicitly stated below.

## CAMERA

The camera is a waterproof action camera rigidly strapped to the rider's own chest. It points forward and slightly down, along the rider's own body, so the rider's legs and feet run away from the camera toward the centre of the frame.

Use an ultra-wide fisheye lens of roughly 150 degrees with strong barrel distortion. The horizon is visibly BOWED: it curves downward at the left and right edges of the frame and lifts in the middle. Straight edges near the frame border bend outward. This bowing is present in every single frame.

The camera is bolted to the rider's torso and never moves independently of it. There is no pan, no tilt, no zoom, no dolly, no gimbal smoothing, no cinematic stabilisation. When the slide banks, the ENTIRE WORLD rotates around the rider's legs while the legs stay in the same place in the frame. When the slide drops, the horizon is thrown up and out of the top of the frame.

The camera NEVER turns back to look at the rider. No face is ever visible. No selfie angle. No mirror. No reflection showing a face. No third-person view. No drone view. No exterior shot of the slide. No shot of the camera itself.

Keep real action-camera artefacts: rolling-shutter skew in fast rotation, auto-exposure pumping when the frame swings from dark city to bright sky, lens flare from the neon strips, water beads sitting on the lens and streaking away, brief blur when spray hits the glass, smeared real-time motion blur, compressed highlights.

## RIDER

The only visible part of the rider is the lower body, and it is visible in every frame.

Two legs run from the bottom edge of the frame toward the centre, converging in perspective. Two bare feet sit at the end of them, close together, soles away from the camera, toes pointing up and forward. The skin is light tan, wet, and shines with the reflected colour of the slide's neon strips. The legs are symmetrical around the vertical centre line of the frame.

The rider wears <<LEGWEAR>>, soaked and reflecting the neon. Nothing else is visible.

NEVER show hands, arms, shoulders, torso, hair, or a face. There is no other person anywhere in the shot.

THE TWO LEGS ARE ALWAYS TWO. In every single frame the left leg and the right leg are separately readable, with a visible gap of slide surface or background between them, and BOTH bare feet are separately visible. They never merge into one dark mass, never collapse into a single silhouette, never lose a foot. This holds hardest during hard banks, full rolls and impacts, which is exactly when it must not fail. The pair stays symmetrical about the vertical centre line of the frame.

A thin bright <<NEON>> edge-light runs down the inner edge of each leg, so the two legs are separated by light and never by darkness alone. The gap between them is filled with what lies beyond: clear slide and city glow, never foam and never black.

The legs react physically and continuously: toes flex and splay on a drop, ankles roll on a bank, knees lift slightly on impact, the whole lower body slides a few centimetres left or right in a hard turn and settles back. The legs are never rigid and never still.

## SLIDE

The slide is a fully TRANSPARENT clear acrylic half-pipe, wide enough for one rider. Its solid walls rise on both sides from beside the rider's hips and curve up into the lower left and lower right corners of the frame, so the tube's cross-section is readable in every frame. You look straight THROUGH its floor and see the empty air and the city directly beneath the rider's feet at all times. This transparency is the single most important element in the shot and must never be lost.

EVERY GLOWING LINE IN THE SHOT IS A PHYSICAL EDGE OF THIS SOLID TUBE. Both upper rims of the half-pipe carry a continuous <<NEON>> electroluminescent strip built into the acrylic itself. There are exactly TWO such strips, one per rim, and the solid surface they bound is visible between them at all times. They run away from the camera and converge at the vanishing point ahead. They light the water, the acrylic and the rider's wet skin from below and from the sides. Their colour is the dominant colour of the entire frame.

Every glowing line in the frame belongs to this tube and continues into the distance along it. If a line glows, the rider can ride on it. Nothing glows in open air away from the slide surface.

A thin sheet of water runs down the slide with the rider. It fans out around the hips, throws spray up past the shins, and hits the lens. The faster the ride, the more spray. The water is lit from beneath by the neon strips.

The slide is one continuous physical object. Every curve, drop, dip and turn is visible AHEAD in the distance before the rider reaches it, grows continuously during the approach, and is then actually travelled. The slide never appears out of nowhere, never changes shape, never breaks and never reconnects.

## MOTION AND GRAVITY

THE RIDER IS ALWAYS MOVING FORWARD AND DOWNWARD AND ALWAYS ACCELERATING. On the slide the ride never stops, never slows to a halt, never reverses and never drifts weightlessly. This holds right up to the moment of impact with the water. The seconds in the pool after the impact are the only part of the video in which the rider is not accelerating.

THE APPROACH RULE applies to everything: every tower, rooftop, bridge, cloud layer and the landing pool must already be visible in the distance before it is reached, must grow continuously as the rider closes on it, must physically pass alongside or beneath the slide, and must recede behind. Something new must already be visible beyond it before it passes. Nothing appears suddenly. Nothing dissolves. Nothing morphs. Nothing teleports. Geography only ever changes through connected physical travel.

Speed is readable at every moment through three things at once: the rate at which the neon edge strips stream past the frame edges, the increasing volume and spread of the spray, and the parallax between near rooftops and the far city.

The whole ride is one falling arc: it starts at the highest point of the city and ends with the impact into the landing pool. Every second on the slide is lower and faster than the one before it. After the impact the rider goes under, then rises and breaks the surface, and the video ends there.

## WORLD

<<CITY>>, real, recognisable and photorealistic, seen from the height of <<LANDMARK>>. The city is present in the frame at all times, either beyond the slide, beside it, or straight down through its transparent floor.

Streets read as continuous rivers of warm amber vehicle and streetlight glow. Building windows are small and individually lit. The scale is honest: the rider is high enough that individual cars are single points of light and whole city blocks fit between the rider's feet.

The weather is <<WEATHER>>, and it is present on every surface in the frame. Real atmospheric depth: distant districts fade into haze, near surfaces are sharp and wet.

## AUDIO

No music. Ever. No soundtrack, no score, no beat, no sound-design swell.

Raw waterproof action-camera microphone, close to the rider's chest, slightly clipped and compressed. Wind roar rises continuously with speed and never dips. Water rushes and hisses directly under the rider's body. The acrylic tube rumbles and squeaks. Spray slaps the lens housing.

ONE female voice, the rider herself, mouth close to the camera on her own chest, breathless and unscripted, reacting in real time. She gasps, swears under her breath, laughs from panic, and screams. Her voice is always processed by the same cheap mic as everything else: never studio-clean, never dubbed, never mixed on top.

The overall loudness climbs steadily from the top of the ride to the splash and never drops back. There is no silence and no fade anywhere in the clip.
