## FORMAT

Create a single continuous <<DURATION>>-second vertical 9:16 video at <<COZUNURLUK>>, <<FPS>> frames per second. Photorealistic live-action action-camera footage, not animation, not a render, not a game. Record the WHOLE video as ONE SINGLE UNBROKEN CONTINUOUS TAKE from the start to the end. There is no edit anywhere. The footage must feel like a real waterproof action camera recording one real ride in real time.

The video begins EXACTLY on the provided starting image: the rider walking barefoot, mid-stride, along a narrow walkway of clear glass high above <<LANDMARK>> in <<CITY>>, her two separate bare legs and feet stepping forward in the lower half of the frame, the open mouth of the transparent slide with its two <<NEON>> rims just a few steps ahead, diving down toward a sea of cloud. From that image she is already walking. The camera, the legs and the slide keep exactly the look of that image for the whole video.

## INDEPENDENCE NOTE

This prompt is the complete video. It does not continue from or lead into any other video. The entire ride, from the first barefoot steps toward the slide high above <<LANDMARK>> to the disaster that buries the camera, happens inside this one generation. Everything required to reconstruct the camera, the rider's body, the slide, the place, the motion and the sound is explicitly stated below.

## CAMERA

The camera is a waterproof action camera rigidly strapped to the rider's own chest. It points forward and steeply down, along the rider's own body, so the rider's legs and feet run away from the camera toward the centre of the frame.

Use an ultra-wide fisheye lens of roughly 150 degrees with strong barrel distortion. The horizon is visibly BOWED: it curves downward at the left and right edges of the frame and lifts in the middle. This bowing is present in every single frame.

The camera is bolted to the rider's torso and never moves independently of it. There is no pan, no tilt, no zoom, no dolly, no gimbal smoothing, no cinematic stabilisation. During the walk-in the frame bobs gently with each step, exactly like a chest camera on a walking person; that bob ends the moment she sits. When the slide banks, the ENTIRE WORLD rotates around the rider's legs while the legs stay in the same place in the frame. When the slide drops, the horizon is thrown up and out of the top of the frame.

The camera NEVER turns back to look at the rider. The rider's face is never visible. No mirror. No view from outside the rider. No exterior shot of the slide. The camera itself, its mount and its strap are never visible.

Keep real action-camera artefacts: rolling-shutter skew in fast rotation, auto-exposure pumping when the frame swings from dark cloud to bright sky, lens flare, water beads, grit and debris sitting on the lens and streaking away, brief blur when spray hits the glass, smeared real-time motion blur.

## RIDER

The only visible part of the rider is the lower body, and it is visible in every frame.

During the walk-in, the first two seconds, the camera looks down along her body at her two bare legs and feet stepping forward one after the other on the clear glass walkway, left, right, left, each wet sole landing flat with a small splash. From the moment she sits in the slide, two legs run from the bottom edge of the frame toward the centre, converging in perspective. Two bare feet sit at the end of them, close together, soles away from the camera, toes pointing up and forward. The skin is light tan, wet, and shines with the reflected colour of the slide's <<NEON>> strips. The legs are symmetrical around the vertical centre line of the frame.

The rider wears <<LEGWEAR>>, soaked. Nothing else is visible. The feet and ankles are completely bare: nothing on them, no straps, no shoes, no sandals, no bindings, nothing tying the legs together.

NEVER show hands, arms, shoulders, torso, hair, or a face. There is no other person anywhere in the shot.

THE TWO LEGS ARE ALWAYS TWO. In every single frame the left leg and the right leg are separately readable, with a visible gap between them, and BOTH bare feet are separately visible. They never merge into one dark mass and never lose a foot, not inside the cloud and not inside the disaster.

A thin bright <<NEON>> edge-light runs down the inner edge of each leg, so the two legs are separated by light and never by darkness alone.

The legs react physically and continuously: toes flex and splay on a drop, ankles roll on a bank, knees lift slightly on impact, the whole lower body slides a few centimetres left or right in a hard turn and settles back.

## SLIDE

The slide is a fully TRANSPARENT clear acrylic half-pipe, wide enough for one rider. Its solid walls rise on both sides from beside the rider's hips and curve up into the lower left and lower right corners of the frame. You look straight THROUGH its floor and see the empty air and the land directly beneath the rider's feet at all times. This transparency is the single most important element in the shot and must never be lost.

Both upper rims of the half-pipe carry a continuous <<NEON>> electroluminescent strip built into the acrylic itself. There are exactly TWO such strips, one per rim. They run away from the camera and converge at the vanishing point ahead. They are lit in EVERY SINGLE FRAME from the start to the end, at full strength, inside the cloud and inside the disaster. Every glowing line in the frame is one of these two rims. Nothing glows in open air: there is no glowing bar across the frame, no web of extra lines, no third strip.

A thin sheet of water runs down the slide with the rider. It fans out around the hips, throws spray up past the shins, and hits the lens. The faster the ride, the more spray.

The slide is one continuous physical object. Every drop and turn is visible AHEAD before the rider reaches it, grows continuously during the approach, and is then actually travelled. It never appears out of nowhere and never breaks.

## MOTION AND GRAVITY

THE WALK-IN RULE: the video opens in motion. From the very first frame the rider is already walking briskly barefoot along a narrow, flat walkway of clear glass high in the air, toward the open mouth of the slide a few steps ahead. The walkway has no side walls and no railing. Through the glass under her feet there is only empty air and the land far below. She never stands still and never pauses at the edge: with every step the slide mouth grows continuously, and at about 2.0 seconds she reaches it, sits straight down into it feet first and pushes off in one fluid movement. The walk lasts about two seconds and never longer.

From the moment she sits, THE RIDER IS ALWAYS MOVING FORWARD AND DOWNWARD AND ALWAYS ACCELERATING, until the end. The slide pitches steeply DOWN, like the first plunge of a giant water slide, and keeps plunging, banking hard left and right between drops so the whole world tilts around the legs. Near objects rush past close to the slide so the speed is obvious. There is no waiting at the edge. The ride never stops, never slows to a halt, never reverses and never floats.

THE APPROACH RULE: every cloud bank, building, rock, wave, storm and slope must already be visible ahead before it is reached, must grow continuously as the rider closes on it, and must pass alongside or beneath the slide. Nothing appears suddenly. Nothing teleports.

THE CLOUD GATE RULE: the cloud bank the slide dives into separates two worlds. Above the cloud is <<WEATHER>>. Inside the cloud the frame becomes an even, flat, featureless grey-white murk in which only the two legs, both feet and the two <<NEON>> rims stay readable, for about two seconds and never longer. Then the slide drops out of the underside of the cloud and the SAME place lies below, caught in <<FELAKET>>. The change happens only inside the cloud, never in clear view.

## WORLD

<<CITY>>, real, recognisable and photorealistic, seen from high above and in front of <<LANDMARK>>. In every clear section of the ride <<LANDMARK>> stands beyond the rider's feet, near the centre of the frame, so the viewer always knows exactly where they are. It stays recognisable even inside the disaster.

Above the cloud the weather is <<WEATHER>>: calm, bright and beautiful. Below the cloud the same place is caught in <<FELAKET>>. The disaster world is darker, harder and more contrasted than the opening world: dark sky, dark debris and one bright hazard, so every threat reads clearly against its background. Real atmospheric depth and honest scale.

## AUDIO

No music. Ever. No soundtrack, no score, no beat.

Raw waterproof action-camera microphone, close to the rider's chest, slightly clipped and compressed. During the walk-in, wet bare footsteps slap on the glass and the high wind is already loud. Wind roar rises continuously with speed. Water hisses directly under the rider's body. The acrylic tube rumbles and creaks. Above the cloud the air sounds open and calm; inside the cloud it goes muffled; below the cloud the disaster roars, and that roar grows louder and closer every second until the end.

THE RIDER NEVER SPEAKS. No words in any language. No talking, no narration, no counting, no exclamations made of words. Her only sounds are breath and wordless voice from the same cheap chest microphone: a sharp gasp, ragged panting, a held breath, short wordless screams.

The overall loudness climbs from the start to the end.
