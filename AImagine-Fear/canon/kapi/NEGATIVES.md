# NEGATIVE BLOCK , KAPI KONSEPTI

Bu blok `KONSEPT: kapi` rotalarinin promptunun EN SONUNA aynen eklenir.

## NEGATIVE

NO music of any kind. NO soundtrack, score or beat.
NO spoken words, NO talking, NO narration, NO dialogue in any language. Only gasps, breathing and wordless screams.
NO edit, NO transition effect, NO jump in time. The clip is one unbroken take.
NO face, head or hair of the rider or of any living person. NO hands. NO arms. NO shoulders. NO torso. NO mirror or reflection showing a person. Carved stone monuments are landscape, not people, and stay fully visible.
NO second rider. NO other people anywhere in the frame.
NO drone view. NO aerial camera. NO exterior view of the slide. NO action camera, chest mount, strap or harness visible in the frame.
NO pan, NO tilt, NO zoom, NO dolly, NO orbit, NO gimbal smoothing, NO camera that moves independently of the rider's body.
NO opaque slide. NO solid floor under the rider. NO glowing line that leaves the slide surface.
NO slow motion. NO speed ramp. NO frozen moment. NO bullet time.
NO on-screen text. NO caption. NO watermark. NO logo. NO UI.
NO flat midday lighting.
NO morphing, NO teleporting geography, NO change of world in clear view: the world changes only inside the cloud.
NO cartoon, NO 3D render look, NO video-game look, NO CGI sheen, NO anime, NO illustration.
NO warped or duplicated feet. NO extra limbs. NO more than two legs. NO more than two feet. NO legs fused into one shape.
NO straps, bindings, shoes or sandals on the feet or ankles.
NO ride that stops, slows to a halt, reverses or floats.
NO landing pool. NO splash-down into a pool. NO happy ending.
NO silence.
