## BEATS

[0.0-2.0] The rider stands still at the terrace edge and the camera on her chest holds the drop dead centre. Her toes press into warm dry whitewash while the air off the caldera is cool. The transparent slide mouth waits ahead of her, its rose gold rims the only saturated colour in the frame. Below her feet there is no sea, only the flat pale top of the mist held inside the bowl, with the far rim of the caldera standing up around it on every side. She does not move yet. The stillness is the fear.

[2.0-4.0] She edges forward toward the mouth in short unsteady steps. The mouth grows continuously and never jumps in size: first a small outline, then a pair of upswept transparent rims taller than her shins, then a wide open throat. Behind it the mist keeps its exact place in the frame and the slide is visible entering it far below, a rose gold line going into pale white. Her legs shake visibly.

[4.0-6.0] She sits and drops into the throat of the slide. The terrace leaves the frame entirely and does not come back. The two rose gold rims snap into hard converging perspective, running away from her hips toward the pale surface below, and the solid clear floor between them is readable the whole way down. The first steep pitch takes her straight at the mist and the top of the mist rises to meet her feet.

[6.0-7.8] She goes into the mist and the caldera closes over her. White closes around the slide on both sides and the rim lights are gone, and because the bowl is closed the white has no far edge at all in any direction: not a layer she is passing through, a room she is inside. The clear floor under her legs stays transparent and the lamps of the cliff villages keep reading through it, dimmer but never absent, a scattered warm line curving under her feet. The two rose gold rims burn at exactly the same brightness they had in open air, converging ahead into white with no end visible. Water beads crawl sideways across the lens. Her legs are the only hard edges in the frame.

[7.8-10.0] She comes out of the bottom of the mist and the Aegean is suddenly close. Black water lies directly beneath the clear floor, dead flat, with the dark curved wall of the caldera rising on one side and the lights of the village strung along its top edge far above. The slide bottoms out and a wall of white water bursts up around her hips and across the lens, lit rose gold from beneath by the rims, then clears in streaks. Speed becomes obvious: the rims are streaming past the frame edges.

[10.0-12.5] The slide banks hard into one long continuous curve carried on thin struts, already visible ahead before she reaches it. The whole caldera rotates around her legs while her legs stay in the same place in the frame. The lit cliff tilts up one side and the black sea fills the other. Spray flies outward along the wall of the turn. Coming out of it the horizon settles far lower and the terrace she started from is a white speck on the rim above the mist behind her.

[12.5-15.0] Straight ahead and growing, a long rose gold-lit infinity pool cut into the lower cliff waits with its far edge open to the sea. The final chute aims at it. She hits the water, the frame goes to churning white for a beat, then punches through into the pool: bright, bubble-filled, muffled under the surface. She rises and breaks through at the open edge, where the water runs over the lip and falls away into the dark.
