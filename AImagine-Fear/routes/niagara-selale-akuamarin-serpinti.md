## OPENING STATE

The rider stands barefoot on the observation deck of the Skylon Tower above Niagara Falls, at night. The weather is a cold night with the mist column from the falls standing in the air. Her two bare legs and bare feet fill the bottom third of the frame, wet and shining on wet steel grating, and the grating itself is trembling: the falls are moving the whole tower a little and it comes up through her heels. Beyond the deck edge there is no ground at all: a thick column of mist stands in the air below her, lit from inside by the coloured floodlights aimed at the water. Far ahead on the deck, small in the distance, sits the open mouth of a fully transparent acrylic slide, its two upswept rims outlined by a continuous aquamarine strip. It runs off the deck edge and down into the mist. There is no railing anywhere between her feet and the drop.

## BEATS

[0.0-2.0] The rider stands still at the deck edge and the camera on her chest holds the drop dead centre. Her toes grip the cold wet grating and the vibration never stops. The transparent slide mouth waits ahead of her, its aquamarine rims the only saturated colour in the frame. Below her feet there is no ground, only the standing mist column with green and white light moving around inside it. She does not move yet. The stillness is the fear, and the noise is already enormous.

[2.0-4.0] She edges forward toward the mouth in short unsteady steps. The mouth grows continuously and never jumps in size: first a small outline, then a pair of upswept transparent rims taller than her shins, then a wide open throat. Behind it the mist column keeps its exact place in the frame and the slide is visible entering it far below, an aquamarine line going into churning white. Her legs shake visibly.

[4.0-6.0] She sits and drops into the throat of the slide. The tower leaves the frame entirely and does not come back. The two aquamarine rims snap into hard converging perspective, running away from her hips toward the white below, and the solid clear floor between them is readable the whole way down. The first steep pitch takes her straight at the mist and the top of the column rises to meet her feet.

[6.0-7.8] She goes into the mist and it hits like weather, not like cloud. White closes around the slide on both sides and the gorge is gone. This mist is heavy and loud: it lands on her legs in real drops, it soaks the lens in a second, and the roar goes from loud to total. The clear floor under her legs stays transparent and the coloured floodlights keep reading through it, dimmer but never absent, green and white sliding under her feet. The two aquamarine rims burn at exactly the same brightness they had in open air, converging ahead into white with no end visible. Her legs are the only hard edges in the frame.

[7.8-10.0] She comes out of the bottom of the mist and Niagara is suddenly close. The gorge opens directly beneath the clear floor, and to one side the whole lit curtain of the Horseshoe Falls stands up green and white, falling beside her at her own speed. The slide bottoms out and a wall of white water bursts up around her hips and across the lens, lit aquamarine from beneath by the rims, then clears in streaks. Speed becomes obvious: the rims are streaming past the frame edges.

[10.0-12.5] The slide banks hard into one long continuous curve carried on thin struts, already visible ahead before she reaches it. The whole gorge rotates around her legs while her legs stay in the same place in the frame. The lit curtain of the falls swings across one side and the black river fills the other. Spray flies outward along the wall of the turn and mixes with the spray already in the air. Coming out of it the horizon settles far lower and the tower she started from is a thin lit stalk above the mist behind her.

[12.5-15.0] Straight ahead and growing, a long aquamarine-lit pool on the Table Rock terrace waits with its far edge open to the gorge. The final chute aims at it. She hits the water, the frame goes to churning white for a beat, then punches through into the pool: bright, bubble-filled, and for the first time in the whole ride the roar is muffled under the surface. She rises and breaks through at the open edge, where the water runs over the lip and falls away into the dark.

## END STATE

The rider hangs at the open overflow edge of an aquamarine-lit pool on the Table Rock terrace at Niagara Falls, both forearms on the wet lip, water pouring over it past her and dropping away into the black gorge below. Her two bare feet trail behind her in the pool, breaking the surface in the lower third of the frame. The bowed fisheye horizon holds the lit green curtain of the Horseshoe Falls close on one side and, far above and behind, the mist column she came down through with the lit ring of the Skylon Tower standing clear above it.
