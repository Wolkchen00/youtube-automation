## BEATS

[0.0-2.0] From the very first moment the rider is already walking briskly barefoot along the narrow glass walkway toward the slide, left foot, right foot, never stopping. Through the clear glass under her feet there is only empty air and the land far below. The open mouth of the slide grows with every step, its two electric turquoise rims curving away and down toward the cloud, and the Horseshoe Falls waits far below and ahead beyond it. She never stands still and never hesitates.

[2.0-4.0] She reaches the mouth, sits straight down into it feet first and pushes off, and the slide takes her at once into the steep plunge. The Horseshoe Falls fills the centre of the frame, the white curtain of water lit orange by the burning sky. The slide drops past the brink of the falls close enough to see the green water pouring over the edge, the spray and the wet rocks rush up and past on the left, the mist and the river far below. Below, the white sea of cloud rushes up toward her and the slide dives straight into it.

[4.0-6.0] The slide plunges into the cloud. The frame becomes an even, flat grey-white murk; only her two legs, both feet and the two electric turquoise rims stay readable. The air turns hot and dry, the murk inside the cloud glows orange from somewhere above, and a deep rumble shakes the acrylic.

[6.0-8.5] She drops out of the underside of the cloud into the same falls under a burning sky. The gorge lies under a red-black sky lit by the falling fireball, the Horseshoe Falls glowing orange through its mist. Burning fragments of the meteor are already raining down, each one hitting the river in a flash and a fountain of steam and fire, and the main fireball is still coming down beyond the falls, growing brighter.

[8.5-10.5] A burning fragment slams into the river right beside the slide, a column of steam and fire blasting into the air, and the shock wave throws the slide sideways. Hot ash and sparks streak across the frame, the acrylic creaks, and grit rattles over the clear floor under her feet. The trees along the cliff burst into flame.

[10.5-13.0] The fireball hits beyond the falls and the blast wave rolls down the gorge toward the slide from the right side of the frame, a towering red-black wall of fire, steam and smoke higher than the falls, with burning trees, rocks and spray inside it, growing every second and filling more and more of the frame.

[13.0-15.0] The slide runs straight into it, both legs and both feet still separately visible and the two electric turquoise rims still burning. The blast wave slams into the camera: fire and steam blast across the lens, the frame burns orange-white for an instant, then dims to near black as ash and soot cover the lens.
