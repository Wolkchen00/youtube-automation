She never stands still and never hesitates.

[2.0-4.0] She reaches the mouth, sits straight down into it feet first and pushes off, and the slide takes her at once into the steep plunge. Table Mountain fills the centre of the frame, its flat grey top sharp against the black smoke pouring over it, the line of fire glowing on its slopes. Below, the white sea of cloud rushes up toward her and the slide dives straight into it.

[4.0-6.0] The slide plunges into the cloud. The frame becomes an even, flat grey-white murk; only her two legs, both feet and the two electric aqua rims stay readable. The air turns hot and smoky, the murk inside the cloud glows orange, and flakes of ash start to stick to the lens.
