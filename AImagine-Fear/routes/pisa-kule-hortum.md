## BEATS

[0.0-2.0] From the very first moment the rider is already walking briskly barefoot along the narrow glass walkway toward the slide, left foot, right foot, never stopping. Through the clear glass under her feet there is only empty air and the land far below. The open mouth of the slide grows with every step, its two neon lavender rims curving away and down toward the cloud, and the Leaning Tower of Pisa waits far below and ahead beyond it. She never stands still and never hesitates.

[2.0-4.0] She reaches the mouth, sits straight down into it feet first and pushes off, and the slide takes her at once into the steep plunge. The Leaning Tower fills the centre of the frame, leaning hard to one side, its white marble galleries gold in the sun under the black storm coming in over the fields. The slide drops past the top of the tower close enough to see the bells in the open belfry and the worn marble columns rush up and past on the right, the green lawn and the cathedral far below. Below, the white sea of cloud rushes up toward her and the slide dives straight into it.

[4.0-6.0] The slide plunges into the cloud. The frame becomes an even, flat grey-white murk; only her two legs, both feet and the two neon lavender rims stay readable. The warm air turns cold and green, a low howl rises inside the cloud, and grit and torn leaves start to whip across the lens.

[6.0-8.5] She drops out of the underside of the cloud into the same square under a green-black supercell. The sky over Pisa has turned a sick dark green, the grass of the Piazza dei Miracoli is flattened by the wind, and the Leaning Tower stands pale against the storm. Beyond the cathedral the giant tornado is grinding across the city toward the square, a black funnel wider than the cathedral, roofs bursting into the air around its base.

[8.5-10.5] Roof tiles, branches and cafe chairs fly past the slide, the wind slams the acrylic sideways and it groans, and dust and rain streak over the clear floor under her feet. The tornado rips the dome off the baptistery and throws the marble into the air, and a flash of lightning lights the leaning tower for an instant.

[10.5-13.0] The tornado crosses the square toward the slide from the right side of the frame, a roaring black wall of wind and dust taller than the Leaning Tower, with roof tiles, trees and broken marble flying inside it, growing every second and filling more and more of the frame.

[13.0-15.0] The slide runs straight into it, both legs and both feet still separately visible and the two neon lavender rims still burning. The tornado swallows the camera: dust and debris blast across the lens, the frame turns dark green-grey, then dims to near black as mud and grit cover the lens.
