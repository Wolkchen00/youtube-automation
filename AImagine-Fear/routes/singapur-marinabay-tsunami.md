## BEATS

[0.0-2.0] From the very first moment the rider is already walking briskly barefoot along the narrow glass walkway toward the slide, left foot, right foot, never stopping. Through the clear glass under her feet there is only empty air and the land far below. The open mouth of the slide grows with every step, its two molten orange rims curving away and down toward the cloud, and Marina Bay Sands waits far below and ahead beyond it. She never stands still and never hesitates.

[2.0-4.0] She reaches the mouth, sits straight down into it feet first and pushes off, and the slide takes her at once into the steep plunge. Marina Bay Sands fills the centre of the frame, the three towers and the SkyPark on top shining in the sun under the dark wall of water rising out at sea. The slide drops past the edge of the SkyPark close enough to see the long pool, the palm trees and the sun loungers rush up and past on the right, the bay and the Supertrees far below. Below, the white sea of cloud rushes up toward her and the slide dives straight into it.

[4.0-6.0] The slide plunges into the cloud. The frame becomes an even, flat grey-white murk; only her two legs, both feet and the two molten orange rims stay readable. The air turns cold and salty, a deep rumble rises inside the cloud, and sea spray starts to bead across the lens.

[6.0-8.5] She drops out of the underside of the cloud into the same city as the tsunami arrives. The bay has drained to wet mud and stranded boats, and Marina Bay Sands stands under a dark grey sky. Out past the towers the giant wave is coming in across the strait, a moving green-black cliff of sea water taller than the towers, ships and cranes tumbling on its face.

[8.5-10.5] The first surge pours over the waterfront and fills the bay in seconds, smashing the boats against the ArtScience Museum and flooding through the Supertrees. Spray blasts up past the slide and hits the lens, and sea water streams over the clear floor under her feet. The SkyPark shudders as the water climbs the towers.

[10.5-13.0] The main wave rolls across the bay toward the slide from the left side of the frame, a towering green-black wall of sea water higher than the three towers, with ships, containers and broken palm trees inside it, its white crest tipping forward, growing every second and filling more and more of the frame.

[13.0-15.0] The slide runs straight into it, both legs and both feet still separately visible and the two molten orange rims still burning. The wave crashes over the camera: foaming sea water slams across the lens, the frame floods green-black, then dims to near black as the water closes over the lens and drags it under.
