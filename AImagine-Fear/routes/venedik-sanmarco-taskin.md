## BEATS

[0.0-2.0] From the very first moment the rider is already walking briskly barefoot along the narrow glass walkway toward the slide, left foot, right foot, never stopping. Through the clear glass under her feet there is only empty air and the land far below. The open mouth of the slide grows with every step, its two sapphire blue rims curving away and down toward the cloud, and St. Mark's Square waits far below and ahead beyond it. She never stands still and never hesitates.

[2.0-4.0] She reaches the mouth, sits straight down into it feet first and pushes off, and the slide takes her at once into the steep plunge. St. Mark's Square fills the centre of the frame, the Campanile rising high above the rooftops, the long arcades of the Doge's Palace and the gold mosaics of the basilica glowing in the low sun. The slide drops past the top of the Campanile close enough to see the gold angel, the green copper spire and the white stone arches of the belfry rush up and past on the right, the gondolas tiny on the quay far below. Below, the white sea of cloud rushes up toward her and the slide dives straight into it.

[4.0-6.0] The slide plunges into the cloud. The frame becomes an even, flat grey-white murk; only her two legs, both feet and the two sapphire blue rims stay readable. The air turns cold and wet, a hard wind howls inside the cloud, the murk darkens to grey-green, and salt spray beads across the lens.

[6.0-8.5] She drops out of the underside of the cloud into the same city drowning in a storm surge. The lagoon has risen over the quay and St. Mark's Square is a lake of black water under a dark slate sky, the lamps of the arcades glowing amber over it. A gale is driving the sea in from the lagoon, and far out a long grey ridge of water is rising and rolling toward the city.

[8.5-10.5] Waves smash over the waterfront and throw the gondolas against the Doge's Palace, splintering them, and water pours through the arcades. A gust slams the slide sideways and the acrylic groans, spray blasts across the frame, and sea water streams over the clear floor under her feet. The amber lamps flicker out one by one as the flood climbs the columns.

[10.5-13.0] The storm surge rolls across the lagoon toward the slide from the left side of the frame, a towering grey-green wall of sea water higher than the arcades, with broken gondolas, mooring poles and shattered wooden boats inside it, its white crest breaking, growing every second and filling more and more of the frame.

[13.0-15.0] The slide runs straight into it, both legs and both feet still separately visible and the two sapphire blue rims still burning. The surge crashes over the camera: foaming sea water slams across the lens, the frame floods grey-green, then dims to near black as the water closes over the lens and drags it under.
