## OPENING STATE

The rider stands barefoot on the narrow maintenance deck at the top of the north tower of the Golden Gate Bridge in San Francisco, at night. The weather is a cold night with the marine fog pouring in through the strait. Her two bare legs and bare feet fill the bottom third of the frame, wet and shining, the red steel cold under her toes. Beyond the deck edge there is no ground at all: a flat grey fog bank lies below her, and the second tower and the downtown skyline push up through it. Far ahead on the deck, small in the distance, sits the open mouth of a fully transparent acrylic slide, its two upswept rims outlined by a continuous molten copper strip. It runs off the tower edge and down into the fog. There is no railing anywhere between her feet and the drop.

## BEATS

[0.0-2.0] The rider stands still at the tower edge and the camera on her chest holds the drop dead centre. Her toes grip the wet rivets. The transparent slide mouth waits ahead of her, its molten copper rims the only saturated colour in the frame. Below her feet there is no bridge and no water, only the flat grey top of the fog bank with the far tower standing up out of it. She does not move yet. The stillness is the fear.

[2.0-4.0] She edges forward toward the mouth in short unsteady steps. The mouth grows continuously and never jumps in size: first a small outline, then a pair of upswept transparent rims taller than her shins, then a wide open throat. Behind it the fog bank keeps its exact place in the frame and the slide is visible entering it far below, a copper line going into grey. Her legs shake visibly.

[4.0-6.0] She sits and drops into the throat of the slide. The tower leaves the frame entirely and does not come back. The two molten copper rims snap into hard converging perspective, running away from her hips toward the grey surface below, and the solid clear floor between them is readable the whole way down. The first steep pitch takes her straight at the fog and the top of the fog rises to meet her feet.

[6.0-7.8] She goes into the fog. Grey closes around the slide on both sides and the skyline is gone. The clear floor under her legs stays transparent and the orange deck lights of the bridge keep reading through it, dimmer but never absent, a long warm smear moving under her feet. One main cable passes close on the left as a dark curve and is swallowed again. The two copper rims burn at exactly the same brightness they had in open air, converging ahead into grey with no end visible. Water beads crawl sideways across the lens. Her legs are the only hard edges in the frame.

[7.8-10.0] She comes out of the bottom of the fog and the strait is suddenly close. Black water runs directly beneath the clear floor, streaked by the bridge lights, with the lit block of Alcatraz to one side and the downtown towers stacked ahead. The slide bottoms out and a wall of white water bursts up around her hips and across the lens, lit copper from beneath by the rims, then clears in streaks. Speed becomes obvious: the rims are streaming past the frame edges.

[10.0-12.5] The slide banks hard into one long continuous curve carried on thin struts, already visible ahead before she reaches it. The whole bay rotates around her legs while her legs stay in the same place in the frame. The lit city tilts up one side and the black water fills the other. Spray flies outward along the wall of the turn. Coming out of it the horizon settles far lower and the tower she started from is a thin red line above the fog behind her.

[12.5-15.0] Straight ahead and growing, a long copper-lit pool on the pier deck at Fort Point waits with its far edge open to the strait. The final chute aims at it. She hits the water, the frame goes to churning white for a beat, then punches through into the pool: bright, bubble-filled, muffled under the surface. She rises and breaks through at the open edge, where the water runs over the lip and falls away into the dark.

## END STATE

The rider hangs at the open overflow edge of a copper-lit pool on the pier deck at Fort Point in San Francisco, both forearms on the wet lip, water pouring over it past her and dropping away into the black strait below. Her two bare feet trail behind her in the pool, breaking the surface in the lower third of the frame. The bowed fisheye horizon holds the downtown skyline and, far above and behind, the fog bank she came down through with the red top of the Golden Gate Bridge tower riding on it.
