## BEATS

[0.0-2.0] From the very first moment the rider is already walking briskly barefoot along the narrow glass walkway toward the slide, left foot, right foot, never stopping. Through the clear glass under her feet there is only empty air and the land far below. The open mouth of the slide grows with every step, its two neon green rims curving away and down toward the cloud, and Christ the Redeemer waits far below and ahead beyond it. She never stands still and never hesitates.

[2.0-4.0] She reaches the mouth, sits straight down into it feet first and pushes off, and the slide takes her at once into the steep plunge. Christ the Redeemer fills the centre of the frame, the pale stone statue standing tall on its pedestal, arms stretched straight out to each side, the folds of its robe sharp in the afternoon sun. The slide drops past its outstretched right hand close enough to see the small pale soapstone tiles and the thin lightning rods on the fingers rush up and past on the left, the green forest dropping away far below. Below, the white sea of cloud rushes up toward her and the slide dives straight into it.

[4.0-6.0] The slide plunges into the cloud. The frame becomes an even, flat grey-white murk; only her two legs, both feet and the two neon green rims stay readable. The air turns hot and heavy, the murk inside the cloud flashes blue-white, and thunder booms so close that the acrylic buzzes under her legs.

[6.0-8.5] She drops out of the underside of the cloud into the same mountain under a tropical lightning storm. Christ the Redeemer stands pale against a black sky, and bolts of blue-white lightning crack down out of the storm and strike the head and the outstretched arms of the statue one after another, each flash lighting it blinding white. Rain pours off the arms in streams and the rainforest below thrashes in the wind.

[8.5-10.5] A bolt strikes the hand of the statue right beside the slide and blasts sparks and chips of stone into the air, the flash burning the frame white for an instant. Thunder shakes the slide, the gusts bend the forest flat, and rain streams over the clear floor under her feet. Behind the statue the black core of the storm is coming over the mountains, swallowing the peaks.

[10.5-13.0] The black storm core rolls over Corcovado toward the slide from the right side of the frame, a towering wall of dark cloud and torrential rain crackling with blue-white bolts, with shredded palm fronds, broken branches and flying leaves inside it, swallowing the arms of the statue, growing every second and filling more and more of the frame.

[13.0-15.0] The slide runs straight into it, both legs and both feet still separately visible and the two neon green rims still burning. A last bolt strikes the slide right in front of the camera: the frame flashes blinding blue-white, rain and sparks blast across the lens, then it dims to near black as the black storm closes over the lens.
