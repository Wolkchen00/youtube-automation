## BEATS

[0.0-2.0] From the very first moment the rider is already walking briskly barefoot along the narrow glass walkway toward the slide, left foot, right foot, never stopping. Through the clear glass under her feet there is only empty air and the land far below. The open mouth of the slide grows with every step, its two sunflower yellow rims curving away and down toward the cloud, and the pyramid of Chichen Itza waits far below and ahead beyond it. She never stands still and never hesitates.

[2.0-4.0] She reaches the mouth, sits straight down into it feet first and pushes off, and the slide takes her at once into the steep plunge. The pyramid of Chichen Itza fills the centre of the frame, its stepped grey terraces sharp in the morning sun, the four staircases climbing to the temple on its summit. The slide drops past the top of the temple close enough to see the carved stone doorway, the weathered limestone blocks and the moss in the cracks rush up and past on the left, the green lawn and the jungle far below. Below, the white sea of cloud rushes up toward her and the slide dives straight into it.

[4.0-6.0] The slide plunges into the cloud. The frame becomes an even, flat grey-white murk; only her two legs, both feet and the two sunflower yellow rims stay readable. The air turns warm, wet and roaring with wind, the murk inside the cloud darkens to steel grey, and rain begins to blast sideways across the lens.

[6.0-8.5] She drops out of the underside of the cloud into the same jungle in the grip of a hurricane. The grey pyramid stands dark under a black sky, the jungle around it thrashing and bending flat in the wind, rain driving sideways in sheets. Beyond it the eyewall of the hurricane is coming: a towering grey wall of rain and wind taller than the pyramid, stretching across the whole horizon and flattening the forest as it comes.

[8.5-10.5] A gust slams the slide sideways and the whole tube shudders and flexes, the acrylic groaning. Whole trees rip out of the ground and tumble across the lawn past the pyramid, palm fronds and branches streak through the air, and rain blasts across the clear floor under her feet. Lightning flashes inside the eyewall and lights the stone terraces steel blue for an instant.

[10.5-13.0] The eyewall reaches the pyramid and swallows the temple on its summit. It rolls straight toward the slide from the right side of the frame, a towering steel-grey wall of driven rain and wind with uprooted trees, broken branches and flying stones inside it, growing every second and filling more and more of the frame.

[13.0-15.0] The slide runs straight into it, both legs and both feet still separately visible and the two sunflower yellow rims still burning. The eyewall slams into the camera: rain and debris blast across the lens, the frame floods steel blue-grey, then dims to near black as mud and shredded leaves plaster over the lens.
