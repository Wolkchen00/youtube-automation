## OPENING STATE

The rider stands barefoot on the open steel platform at the broken glass tip of the Shard in London, at night. The weather is cold drizzle with a low cloud ceiling sitting on the river. Her two bare legs and bare feet fill the bottom third of the frame, wet and shining. She is above the weather: a flat grey cloud ceiling lies under her feet from one edge of the frame to the other, and only the dark silhouettes of the tallest towers push up through it. Nothing of the city is visible yet. Far ahead on the platform, small in the distance, sits the open mouth of a fully transparent acrylic slide, its two upswept rims outlined by a continuous ember orange strip, running off the platform edge and down into the grey. There is no railing anywhere between her feet and the drop.

## BEATS

[0.0-2.0] The rider stands still at the platform edge and the camera on her chest holds the drop dead centre. Her toes flex against the cold wet steel. The transparent slide mouth waits ahead of her, its ember orange rims the only saturated colour in the frame. Below her feet there is no London at all, only the flat grey ceiling of cloud with a few dark tower tops standing out of it. She does not move yet. The stillness is the fear.

[2.0-4.0] She edges forward toward the mouth in short unsteady steps. The mouth grows continuously and never jumps in size: first a small outline, then a pair of upswept transparent rims taller than her shins, then a wide open throat. Behind it the cloud ceiling keeps its exact place in the frame and the slide is visible going into it far below, an orange line entering grey. Her legs shake visibly.

[4.0-6.0] She sits and drops into the throat of the slide. The platform leaves the frame entirely and does not come back. The two ember orange rims snap into hard converging perspective, running away from her hips toward the grey surface below, and the solid clear floor between them is readable the whole way down. The first steep pitch takes her straight at the cloud ceiling and the grey rises to meet her feet.

[6.0-7.8] She goes into the ceiling. Grey closes around the slide and the tower tops are gone. The clear floor under her legs stays transparent and the glow of the streets keeps reading through it, dimmer but never absent, a warm orange wash moving under her feet. The two ember rims burn at exactly the same brightness they had in open air, converging ahead into grey with no end visible. Drizzle beads crawl sideways across the lens. Her legs are the only hard edges in the frame.

[7.8-10.0] She comes out of the underside of the ceiling and London is suddenly close and wide. The black bend of the river runs directly under the clear floor, and the lit stone towers and raised walkways of Tower Bridge sit ahead of her across the water. The slide bottoms out and a wall of white water bursts up around her hips and across the lens, lit ember orange from beneath by the rims, then clears in streaks. Speed becomes obvious: the rims are streaming past the frame edges.

[10.0-12.5] The slide banks hard into one long continuous curve carried on thin struts, already visible ahead before she reaches it. The whole city rotates around her legs while her legs stay in the same place in the frame. The river and the lit stone towers of Tower Bridge tilt up one side of the frame and the wet sky fills the other. Spray flies outward along the wall of the turn. Coming out of it the horizon settles far lower and the tip she started from is hidden, somewhere above the ceiling behind her.

[12.5-15.0] Straight ahead and growing, a long amber-lit pool on a riverside roof waits with brick warehouse walls on three sides of it. The final chute aims at it. She hits the water, the frame goes to churning white for a beat, then punches through into the pool: bright, bubble-filled, muffled under the surface. She rises and breaks through into cold air, with the river running past below the roof edge.

## END STATE

The rider kneels in the shallow end of a long amber-lit pool on a riverside brick roof in London, water running off her shoulders. Her two bare feet trail behind her and break the surface in the lower third of the frame. Water still churns from her entry. The bowed fisheye horizon holds the black river, the lit stone towers of Tower Bridge across the water, and above all of it the unbroken grey ceiling of cloud that the Shard is standing somewhere behind.
