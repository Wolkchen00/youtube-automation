## OPENING STATE

The rider stands barefoot on the timber planks of the Marienbrucke footbridge across the Pollat gorge, facing Neuschwanstein Castle in Bavaria, at night. The weather is a cold night with alpine cloud filling the gorge below the bridge. Her two bare legs and bare feet fill the bottom third of the frame, wet and shining on old wet planks laid with gaps between them, and through those gaps she can already see straight down into the cloud before she has taken a single step. Behind her the castle stands floodlit on its rock, its towers warm against the black forest. Beyond the bridge rail line there is no ground at all: the gorge is packed to the brim with still cloud. Far ahead along the bridge, small in the distance, sits the open mouth of a fully transparent acrylic slide, its two upswept rims outlined by a continuous bronze strip. It runs off the bridge and down into the cloud. There is no railing anywhere between her feet and the drop.

## BEATS

[0.0-2.0] The rider stands still on the planks and the camera on her chest holds the drop dead centre. Her toes grip the edge of a plank and the gap beside them shows white cloud a long way down. The transparent slide mouth waits ahead of her, its bronze rims the only saturated colour in the frame. Below her feet there is no gorge floor, only the flat still top of the cloud filling it. She does not move yet. The stillness is the fear, and the floor is already showing her the fall.

[2.0-4.0] She edges forward toward the mouth in short unsteady steps, and each step lands on a plank with another gap beside it. The mouth grows continuously and never jumps in size: first a small outline, then a pair of upswept transparent rims taller than her shins, then a wide open throat. Behind it the cloud keeps its exact place in the frame and the slide is visible entering it far below, a bronze line going into white. Her legs shake visibly.

[4.0-6.0] She sits and drops into the throat of the slide. The bridge leaves the frame entirely and does not come back. The two bronze rims snap into hard converging perspective, running away from her hips toward the white surface below, and the solid clear floor between them is readable the whole way down. The first steep pitch takes her straight at the cloud and the top of the cloud rises to meet her feet.

[6.0-7.8] She goes into the cloud. White closes around the slide on both sides and the castle is gone. This cloud is cold and dry and completely silent, and the silence after the wind on the bridge is its own kind of wrong. The clear floor under her legs stays transparent and the warm floodlight of the castle keeps reading through it from behind and above, dimmer but never absent, a single amber patch sliding away under her feet. The two bronze rims burn at exactly the same brightness they had in open air, converging ahead into white with no end visible. Water beads crawl sideways across the lens. Her legs are the only hard edges in the frame.

[7.8-10.0] She comes out of the bottom of the cloud and the gorge is suddenly close. Black rock walls stand up on both sides of the clear floor, close enough to read, with the thin white thread of the Pollat river directly beneath her and the flat dark water of the Alpsee opening ahead. The slide bottoms out and a wall of white water bursts up around her hips and across the lens, lit bronze from beneath by the rims, then clears in streaks. Speed becomes obvious: the rims are streaming past the frame edges.

[10.0-12.5] The slide banks hard into one long continuous curve carried on thin struts, already visible ahead before she reaches it. The whole valley rotates around her legs while her legs stay in the same place in the frame. The black forest tilts up one side and the flat lake fills the other. Spray flies outward along the wall of the turn. Coming out of it the horizon settles far lower and the castle is behind her now, not above her, its lit towers riding on the cloud she came through.

[12.5-15.0] Straight ahead and growing, a long bronze-lit pool on a lakeside terrace at the Alpsee waits with its far edge open to the water. The final chute aims at it. She hits the water, the frame goes to churning white for a beat, then punches through into the pool: bright, bubble-filled, muffled under the surface. She rises and breaks through at the open edge, where the water runs over the lip and falls away into the dark.

## END STATE

The rider hangs at the open overflow edge of a bronze-lit pool on a lakeside terrace at the Alpsee in Bavaria, both forearms on the wet lip, water pouring over it past her and dropping away into the black lake below. Her two bare feet trail behind her in the pool, breaking the surface in the lower third of the frame. The bowed fisheye horizon holds the dark treeline and, far above and behind, the cloud-filled gorge she came down through with the floodlit towers of Neuschwanstein Castle standing clear above it.
