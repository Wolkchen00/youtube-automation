## OPENING STATE

The rider stands barefoot at the outer lip of a black glass observation deck on the very top of the MahaNakhon tower in Bangkok, at night. The weather is a hot humid night after the rain, the streets still wet. Her two bare legs and bare feet fill the bottom third of the frame, wet and shining. Beyond the deck edge the frame is filled entirely by Bangkok, seen from vertically above: the wide gold-lit curve of the Chao Phraya river snaking through the city, glowing temple roofs scattered between the towers, and rivers of headlights streaming through the streets. Far ahead on the deck, small in the distance, sits the open mouth of a fully transparent acrylic slide, its two upswept rims outlined by a continuous mango orange electroluminescent strip. There is no railing anywhere between her feet and the drop.

## BEATS

[0.0-2.0] The rider stands still at the edge and the camera on her chest holds the drop dead centre. Her toes flex against the wet black deck. The transparent slide mouth waits ahead of her, its mango orange rims the only saturated colour in the frame. The whole of Bangkok lies directly beneath her feet, the wide gold-lit curve of the Chao Phraya river snaking through the city, glowing temple roofs scattered between the towers, and rivers of headlights streaming through the streets, and nothing separates her from it. She does not move yet. The stillness is the fear.

[2.0-4.0] She edges forward toward the mouth in short unsteady steps. The mouth grows continuously and never jumps in size: first a small outline, then a pair of upswept transparent rims taller than her shins, then a wide open throat. Behind it Bangkok keeps its exact place in the frame, sliding down slightly as she closes on the edge. Her legs shake visibly.

[4.0-6.0] She sits and drops into the throat of the slide. The deck leaves the frame entirely and does not come back. The two mango orange rims snap into hard converging perspective, running away from her hips to a vanishing point far below, and the solid clear floor between them is readable the whole way down. Through it there is nothing but open air and the lights of Bangkok directly under her. The first steep pitch takes her and the horizon is thrown up and out of the top of the frame.

[6.0-8.0] The slide bottoms out and a wall of white water bursts up around her hips and across the lens. Spray floods the frame for a moment, lit mango orange from beneath by the rims, then clears in streaks. Her legs are soaked and both feet stay separately visible through it. The camera pumps its exposure as the frame swings from dark sky to bright water and back. Speed becomes obvious: the two rims are now streaming past the frame edges.

[8.0-10.0] The slide banks hard and the entire city rotates around her legs while her legs stay in the same place in the frame. The wide gold-lit curve of the Chao Phraya river snaking through the city, glowing temple roofs scattered between the towers, and rivers of headlights streaming through the streets tilts up one side of the frame and the night sky fills the other. Ahead and already visible, the slide runs into one long continuous banked curve carried on thin support struts, growing as she closes on it.

[10.0-11.5] She holds that curve and the city rolls all the way around the frame, once, continuously, never cutting and never jumping. Spray flies outward along the wall of the turn. Coming out of it the horizon settles far lower than before, and Bangkok is no longer beneath her but rising around her: the glowing golden roofs of a temple now sits level with the slide.

[11.5-13.0] The slide drops into a canyon between two dark towers. Lit windows stream past on both sides of the frame, close and fast, throwing warm rectangles across the wet acrylic and across her legs. Straight ahead and below, already visible and growing, a rectangular amber-lit rooftop pool waits between the buildings. The final chute aims directly at it.

[13.0-15.0] She hits the water. The frame goes to churning white for a beat, then punches through into the pool: bright, bubble-filled, muffled under the surface. She rises and breaks through. The lens clears in streaks and the last thing in the frame is her two bare feet floating in amber-lit water with the towers of Bangkok standing around the pool.

## END STATE

The rider is floating on her back in a amber-lit rooftop pool between dark towers in Bangkok. Her two bare feet break the surface in the lower third of the frame, wet and lit by the pool lights. Water still churns from her entry. The bowed fisheye horizon is filled with lit building windows and, far above and behind, the MahaNakhon tower she started from.
