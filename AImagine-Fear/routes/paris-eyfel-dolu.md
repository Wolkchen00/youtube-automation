## OPENING STATE

The rider is already walking briskly, barefoot and mid-stride, along a narrow walkway of clear glass high above the Eiffel Tower in Paris, a few steps from the open mouth of the transparent slide. Far below and ahead, filling the upper half of the frame: the Eiffel Tower seen from the FRONT, from across the river, on a soft summer evening, its brown iron lattice glowing warm in the low sun and its four great legs arching over the long green lawns of the Champ de Mars. A thick white cloud bank lies across the city below the slide, and the slide dives down toward it. Her two separate bare legs and bare feet step forward in the lower half of the frame, wet and shining, and just ahead the two electric fuchsia rims of the slide mouth run away and down toward the cloud below. Engraved into the clear acrylic floor of the slide mouth, between the two rims, the name MIGUEL glows in large white capital letters, part of the glass itself and readable from where she walks. There is no railing. The weather is a soft summer evening over the Seine, the sky clear and pale gold.

## BEATS

[0.0-2.0] From the very first moment the rider is already walking briskly barefoot along the narrow glass walkway toward the slide, left foot, right foot, never stopping. Through the clear glass under her feet there is only empty air and the land far below. The open mouth of the slide grows with every step, its two electric fuchsia rims curving away and down toward the cloud, and the Eiffel Tower waits far below and ahead beyond it. The glowing engraved name MIGUEL on the slide floor stays sharp and readable, letter for letter, as she walks toward it. She never stands still and never hesitates.

[2.0-4.0] She reaches the mouth, sits straight down over the glowing name feet first and pushes off, and the slide takes her at once into the steep plunge. The Eiffel Tower fills the centre of the frame, its iron lattice narrowing to the slender top, the three platforms stacked one above the other, the evening sun glinting on every rivet. The slide drops past the very top of the tower close enough to see the criss-crossed brown girders and the bolts rush up and past on the right, the long green lawns of the Champ de Mars far below. Below, the white sea of cloud rushes up toward her and the slide dives straight into it.

[4.0-6.0] The slide plunges into the cloud. The frame becomes an even, flat grey-white murk; only her two legs, both feet and the two electric fuchsia rims stay readable. The air turns suddenly cold, the murk inside the cloud turns a sickly green-grey, and the first pellets of ice crack against the acrylic and bounce off the lens.

[6.0-8.5] She drops out of the underside of the cloud into the same city under a giant hail supercell. The Eiffel Tower stands black against a green-black sky, a vast storm cloud hanging low over Paris with a white curtain of hail pouring from its base. Hailstones the size of golf balls smash onto the rooftops and bounce high off the streets, and the lawns of the Champ de Mars are turning white.

[8.5-10.5] The hail gets bigger. Lumps of ice the size of fists hammer the slide, crack white stars into the acrylic beside her shins and bounce off the rims, and one skids across the clear floor between her feet. A bolt of lightning strikes the top of the Eiffel Tower and lights the whole lattice white for an instant, and windows burst along the boulevards as the ice hits them.

[10.5-13.0] The hail curtain sweeps down the Seine toward the slide from the left side of the frame, a towering white wall of falling ice under the green-black cloud, hiding the bridges and then the legs of the tower, with shattered roof slates, shutters and torn chestnut branches tumbling inside it, growing every second and filling more and more of the frame.

[13.0-15.0] The slide runs straight into it, both legs and both feet still separately visible and the two electric fuchsia rims still burning. The hail wall hits the camera: ice hammers the lens, cracks spread across the glass, the frame goes blinding white, then dims to near black as hailstones pile up over the lens and bury it.

## END STATE

The camera lies buried under a heap of hailstones. The frame is almost black, with only a faint green-white glow through the packed ice pressed against the cracked lens. The drumming of the hail is muffled and fading.
