## OPENING STATE

The rider stands barefoot at the outer end of the open steel skybridge that joins the two Petronas Towers in Kuala Lumpur, at night. The weather is a warm monsoon night with fine rain and low cloud caught between the towers. Her two bare legs and bare feet fill the bottom third of the frame, wet and shining. On both sides of her the two ribbed steel towers rise out of the top of the frame and drop out of the bottom of it, lit silver-white all the way down. Between them, directly under her feet, a wedge of grey cloud sits trapped in the gap and hides the street. Far ahead on the bridge, small in the distance, sits the open mouth of a fully transparent acrylic slide, its two upswept rims outlined by a continuous electric yellow strip. There is no railing anywhere between her feet and the drop.

## BEATS

[0.0-2.0] The rider stands still at the end of the bridge and the camera on her chest holds the gap dead centre. Her toes flex against the wet steel. The transparent slide mouth waits ahead of her, its electric yellow rims the only saturated colour in the frame. The two ribbed towers stand hard on either side of her legs and between them there is only the grey cloud trapped in the gap. She does not move yet. The stillness is the fear.

[2.0-4.0] She edges forward toward the mouth in short unsteady steps. The mouth grows continuously and never jumps in size: first a small outline, then a pair of upswept transparent rims taller than her shins, then a wide open throat. Behind it the two towers keep their exact place in the frame and the slide is visible dropping into the cloud wedge between them. Her legs shake visibly.

[4.0-6.0] She sits and drops into the throat of the slide. The bridge leaves the frame entirely and does not come back. The two electric yellow rims snap into hard converging perspective, running away from her hips down the gap between the towers, and the solid clear floor between them is readable the whole way down. The first steep pitch takes her and the ribbed steel of both towers begins to stream past on both sides of the frame.

[6.0-7.8] She goes into the trapped cloud. Grey closes around the slide and the street is gone. The clear floor under her legs stays transparent and the glow of the traffic keeps reading through it, dimmer but never absent, a moving orange grid under her feet. The two yellow rims burn at exactly the same brightness they had in open air. The ribbed walls of the towers are still there on both sides, close, grey, streaming past faster than before. Rain beads crawl sideways across the lens.

[7.8-10.0] She comes out of the bottom of the cloud and Kuala Lumpur is suddenly close. The lit street grid runs away in every direction under the clear floor and the low roofs of the old shophouses sit directly ahead. The slide bottoms out and a wall of white water bursts up around her hips and across the lens, lit yellow from beneath by the rims, then clears in streaks. Speed becomes obvious: the rims are streaming past the frame edges.

[10.0-12.5] The slide banks hard into one long continuous curve carried on thin struts, already visible ahead before she reaches it. The whole city rotates around her legs while her legs stay in the same place in the frame. The two towers tilt together up one side of the frame, still lit silver-white, and the wet night sky fills the other. Spray flies outward along the wall of the turn. Coming out of it the horizon settles far lower and the skybridge she started from is a thin line high above, back inside the cloud.

[12.5-15.0] Straight ahead and growing, a narrow yellow-lit pool on a low podium roof at the foot of the towers waits between two walls of lit windows. The final chute aims at it. She hits the water, the frame goes to churning white for a beat, then punches through into the pool: bright, bubble-filled, muffled under the surface. She rises and breaks through, and the two towers are standing straight up above her head.

## END STATE

The rider treads water in a narrow yellow-lit pool on a podium roof at the foot of the Petronas Towers in Kuala Lumpur, her head tipped back. Her two bare feet break the surface in the lower third of the frame, wet and lit by the pool lights. Water still churns from her entry. The bowed fisheye horizon is filled by the two ribbed towers going straight up out of the top of the frame on either side, and by the grey cloud still caught in the gap between them far above.
