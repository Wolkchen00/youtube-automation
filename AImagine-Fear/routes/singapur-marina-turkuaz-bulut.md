## OPENING STATE

The rider stands barefoot on the outer lip of the long boat-shaped roof deck laid across the three towers of the Marina Bay Sands in Singapore, at night. The weather is a warm night with a low cloud bank drifting in off the bay. Her two bare legs and bare feet fill the bottom third of the frame, wet and shining. Beyond the deck edge there is no ground at all: a flat white cloud bank lies below her, and the lit towers of the financial district push up through it. Far ahead on the deck, small in the distance, sits the open mouth of a fully transparent acrylic slide, its two upswept rims outlined by a continuous electric turquoise strip. It runs off the deck edge and down into the cloud. There is no railing anywhere between her feet and the drop.

## BEATS

[0.0-2.0] The rider stands still at the deck edge and the camera on her chest holds the drop dead centre. Her toes flex against the wet deck. The transparent slide mouth waits ahead of her, its electric turquoise rims the only saturated colour in the frame. Below her feet there is no city, only the flat white top of the cloud bank with the lit towers of the financial district standing up out of it. She does not move yet. The stillness is the fear.

[2.0-4.0] She edges forward toward the mouth in short unsteady steps. The mouth grows continuously and never jumps in size: first a small outline, then a pair of upswept transparent rims taller than her shins, then a wide open throat. Behind it the cloud bank keeps its exact place in the frame and the slide is visible entering it far below, a turquoise line going into white. Her legs shake visibly.

[4.0-6.0] She sits and drops into the throat of the slide. The deck leaves the frame entirely and does not come back. The two electric turquoise rims snap into hard converging perspective, running away from her hips toward the white surface below, and the solid clear floor between them is readable the whole way down. The first steep pitch takes her straight at the cloud and the top of the cloud rises to meet her feet.

[6.0-7.8] She goes into the cloud. White closes around the slide on both sides and the towers are gone. The clear floor under her legs stays transparent and the glow of the city keeps reading through it, dimmer but never absent, a wide orange smear moving under her feet. The two turquoise rims burn at exactly the same brightness they had in open air, converging ahead into white with no end visible. Water beads crawl sideways across the lens. Her legs are the only hard edges in the frame.

[7.8-10.0] She comes out of the bottom of the cloud and Singapore is suddenly close. The bay is directly beneath the clear floor, black and flat, with the lit ring of the Singapore Flyer to one side and the tall lit supertrees of the gardens ahead. The slide bottoms out and a wall of white water bursts up around her hips and across the lens, lit turquoise from beneath by the rims, then clears in streaks. Speed becomes obvious: the rims are streaming past the frame edges.

[10.0-12.5] The slide banks hard into one long continuous curve carried on thin struts, already visible ahead before she reaches it. The whole bay rotates around her legs while her legs stay in the same place in the frame. The lit ring of the Singapore Flyer tilts up one side and the black water fills the other. Spray flies outward along the wall of the turn. Coming out of it the horizon settles far lower and the roof deck she started from is a thin line above the cloud behind her.

[12.5-15.0] Straight ahead and growing, the long turquoise-lit rooftop pool on the deck of a lower tower waits with its far edge open to the night. The final chute aims at it. She hits the water, the frame goes to churning white for a beat, then punches through into the pool: bright, bubble-filled, muffled under the surface. She rises and breaks through at the open edge, where the water runs over the lip and falls away into the dark.

## END STATE

The rider hangs at the open overflow edge of a turquoise-lit rooftop pool in Singapore, both forearms on the wet lip, water pouring over it past her and dropping away into the dark bay below. Her two bare feet trail behind her in the pool, breaking the surface in the lower third of the frame. The bowed fisheye horizon holds the lit towers of the financial district and, far above and behind, the cloud bank she came down through with the roof deck of the Marina Bay Sands riding on top of it.
