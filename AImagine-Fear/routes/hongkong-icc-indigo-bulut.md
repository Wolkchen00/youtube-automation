## OPENING STATE

The rider stands barefoot on a narrow open ledge at the very top of the ICC tower in Hong Kong, at night. The weather is a humid night with sea fog rolling in off the harbour. Her two bare legs and bare feet fill the bottom third of the frame, wet and shining. Below her the harbour is half swallowed: a thick bank of sea fog lies on the water and the towers of the island across it stand up out of the fog in a wall of coloured light. Far ahead on the ledge, small in the distance, sits the open mouth of a fully transparent acrylic slide, its two upswept rims outlined by a continuous deep indigo strip, running off the ledge and down toward the fog. There is no railing anywhere between her feet and the drop.

## BEATS

[0.0-2.0] The rider stands still at the ledge edge and the camera on her chest holds the drop dead centre. Her toes flex against the wet concrete. The transparent slide mouth waits ahead of her, its deep indigo rims the only saturated colour near her. Far below and ahead, the wall of lit towers on the island stands out of a flat bank of sea fog, and the water between is hidden. She does not move yet. The stillness is the fear.

[2.0-4.0] She edges forward toward the mouth in short unsteady steps. The mouth grows continuously and never jumps in size: first a small outline, then a pair of upswept transparent rims taller than her shins, then a wide open throat. Behind it the fog bank and the lit island keep their exact place in the frame and the slide is visible running down into the fog. Her legs shake visibly.

[4.0-6.0] She sits and drops into the throat of the slide. The ledge leaves the frame entirely and does not come back. The two deep indigo rims snap into hard converging perspective, running away from her hips out over the harbour, and the solid clear floor between them is readable the whole way down. The first steep pitch takes her and the lit island swings up and out of the top of the frame.

[6.0-7.8] She goes into the sea fog. Grey-white closes around the slide and the towers are gone. The clear floor under her legs stays transparent and the coloured glow of the island keeps reading through it, dimmer but never absent, a broad band of red and green moving under her feet. The two indigo rims burn at exactly the same brightness they had in open air, converging ahead into the fog with no end visible. Salt water beads crawl sideways across the lens.

[7.8-10.0] She comes out under the fog and the harbour is right there, close and black and moving. A lit passenger ferry crosses directly beneath the clear floor, its wake spreading out under her legs, and the wall of coloured towers now stands ahead of her at eye level instead of below. The slide bottoms out and a wall of white water bursts up around her hips and across the lens, lit indigo from beneath by the rims, then clears in streaks. The rims are streaming past the frame edges.

[10.0-12.5] The slide banks hard into one long continuous curve carried on thin struts, already visible ahead before she reaches it. The whole harbour rotates around her legs while her legs stay in the same place in the frame. The wall of coloured towers tilts up one side of the frame and the black water fills the other. Spray flies outward along the wall of the turn. Coming out of it the horizon settles far lower, and the tower she started from is gone, hidden above the fog behind her.

[12.5-15.0] Straight ahead and growing, a square indigo-lit pool on a low harbour-front podium waits with the water of the harbour running past just beyond its far wall. The final chute aims at it. She hits the water, the frame goes to churning white for a beat, then punches through into the pool: bright, bubble-filled, muffled under the surface. She rises and breaks through facing the harbour, with the ferry still crossing behind the pool wall.

## END STATE

The rider stands waist deep in a square indigo-lit pool on a harbour-front podium in Hong Kong, facing the water, both hands wiping her eyes. Her two bare feet are visible through the lit water in the lower third of the frame. Water still churns from her entry. The bowed fisheye horizon holds the black harbour, a lit passenger ferry crossing it, the wall of coloured towers on the island behind that, and the flat bank of sea fog sitting over all of it.
