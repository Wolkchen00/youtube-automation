## OPENING STATE

The rider stands barefoot on the deck outside the upper cableway station on the flat summit of Table Mountain in Cape Town, at night. The weather is a mild night with the tablecloth cloud spilling over the flat summit edge. Her two bare legs and bare feet fill the bottom third of the frame, wet and shining on flat wet sandstone slabs. The whole summit is dead level, so the edge arrives with no warning slope at all. The cloud does not sit still: it comes across the table behind her, reaches the lip, and pours downward over it in a slow continuous fall. Beyond that lip there is no ground, only moving white with the dark shoulder of Lion's Head pushing up through it. Far ahead on the deck, small in the distance, sits the open mouth of a fully transparent acrylic slide, its two upswept rims outlined by a continuous emerald green strip. It runs off the lip and down inside the falling cloud. There is no railing anywhere between her feet and the drop.

## BEATS

[0.0-2.0] The rider stands still on the flat slabs and the camera on her chest holds the drop dead centre. Her toes sit in a thin sheet of water running across the stone toward the edge. The transparent slide mouth waits ahead of her, its emerald rims the only saturated colour in the frame. Below her feet there is no city, only the moving white of the cloud going over the lip and down. She does not move yet. The stillness is the fear, and the cloud around her is the only thing moving.

[2.0-4.0] She edges forward toward the mouth in short unsteady steps. The mouth grows continuously and never jumps in size: first a small outline, then a pair of upswept transparent rims taller than her shins, then a wide open throat. Behind it the cloud keeps pouring over the lip at the same steady rate and the slide is visible entering it, an emerald line going into white. Her legs shake visibly.

[4.0-6.0] She sits and drops into the throat of the slide. The summit leaves the frame entirely and does not come back. The two emerald rims snap into hard converging perspective, running away from her hips toward the white below, and the solid clear floor between them is readable the whole way down. The first steep pitch takes her straight into the falling cloud and the white rises to meet her feet.

[6.0-7.8] She goes into the cloud and it is falling with her. White closes around the slide on both sides, but it is not still white: it streams downward past the rims at almost her own speed, so the sense of falling almost disappears and then comes back worse. Lion's Head is gone. The clear floor under her legs stays transparent and the orange grid of Cape Town keeps reading through it, dimmer but never absent. The two emerald rims burn at exactly the same brightness they had in open air, converging ahead into white with no end visible. Water beads crawl sideways across the lens. Her legs are the only hard edges in the frame.

[7.8-10.0] She comes out of the bottom of the cloud and Cape Town is suddenly close. The white peels off the rims in a sheet that keeps going down behind her, and beneath the clear floor lie the pale beaches of Camps Bay and the flat black Atlantic. The slide bottoms out and a wall of white water bursts up around her hips and across the lens, lit emerald from beneath by the rims, then clears in streaks. Speed becomes obvious: the rims are streaming past the frame edges.

[10.0-12.5] The slide banks hard into one long continuous curve carried on thin struts, already visible ahead before she reaches it. The whole coastline rotates around her legs while her legs stay in the same place in the frame. The lit bowl of the city tilts up one side and the black ocean fills the other. Spray flies outward along the wall of the turn. Coming out of it the horizon settles far lower and the flat summit she started from is a long dark bar above the cloud behind her, still pouring over its own edge.

[12.5-15.0] Straight ahead and growing, a long emerald-lit rooftop pool above the Camps Bay beachfront waits with its far edge open to the sea. The final chute aims at it. She hits the water, the frame goes to churning white for a beat, then punches through into the pool: bright, bubble-filled, muffled under the surface. She rises and breaks through at the open edge, where the water runs over the lip and falls away into the dark.

## END STATE

The rider hangs at the open overflow edge of an emerald-lit rooftop pool above the Camps Bay beachfront in Cape Town, both forearms on the wet lip, water pouring over it past her and dropping away into the dark street below. Her two bare feet trail behind her in the pool, breaking the surface in the lower third of the frame. The bowed fisheye horizon holds the black Atlantic on one side and, far above and behind, the long flat top of Table Mountain with the tablecloth cloud still spilling off its edge.
