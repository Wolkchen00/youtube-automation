## OPENING STATE

The rider stands barefoot on the glass floor of the CN Tower observation deck in Toronto at blue-hour dusk. Her two bare feet and short black glossy wet-look shorts fill the bottom third of the frame. Beneath the glass, straight down through it, the city is already visible: a dense grid of lit streets and small towers, and beyond the city Lake Ontario and the long dark spit of the Toronto Islands reaching out into the water. The sky is deep blue, the sun is gone, and a band of thin high cloud lies across a bowed fisheye horizon. Directly ahead on the deck, waiting, is the open mouth of a fully transparent acrylic slide, its two upswept edges outlined by a continuous hot red-orange electroluminescent strip. A second red LED strip runs along the deck edge in front of her feet. The red light is the only warm colour in an otherwise blue frame.

## BEATS

[0.0-2.5] The rider stands motionless on the glass floor and the camera on her chest holds the drop dead centre. Her bare toes press against the cold glass and the whole of Toronto lies directly beneath them, seen through the floor, with the lake and the islands on the bowed horizon beyond. The transparent slide mouth glows red ahead of her. She does not move yet. The stillness is the fear.

[2.5-5.0] She edges forward and sits into the mouth. It grows continuously as she closes on it, from a small red outline to a pair of transparent upswept wings taller than her knees, and never jumps in size. Her legs enter the throat of the slide and the red strips light her wet shins from both sides. The deck leaves the frame and does not come back.

[5.0-7.5] The first pitch takes her. The two red strips snap into hard converging perspective running away below her feet, and through the transparent floor between her legs there is nothing but open air and the lit grid of Toronto directly underneath. The horizon is thrown up and out of the top of the frame. A first burst of white water comes up the left side of the slide and slaps across the lens.

[7.5-10.0] The slide banks hard and the entire city rotates around her legs while her legs stay in the same place in the frame. The lake swings up the left of the frame, the blue sky fills the right, and the amber street grid slides across the bottom. Spray fans out along the whole outer wall of the curve, lit red from underneath. Speed is now unmistakable: the red strips are streaming past the frame edges.

[10.0-12.5] Ahead and above, already visible against the cloud band, the slide climbs into one long continuous banked curve carried on thin red support struts. She rides up into it. For a moment the frame is almost entirely sky and red rail, the city pushed to the bottom edge, and the ride is at its highest and most exposed.

[12.5-15.0] She comes over the top of that curve and the world rolls all the way around the frame once, continuously, without a cut. She punches through the thin cloud layer and the frame goes soft and grey-blue for a beat, then clears. Coming out, the horizon has dropped: the city is much closer and rising toward her, and individual rooftops are now readable.

[15.0-17.5] Through the transparent floor between her feet a rectangular turquoise rooftop pool becomes visible far below, lit from inside, small and growing. The slide straightens into its final chute and aims directly at it. The two red strips converge on the pool. Buildings rise past on both sides. The pool grows continuously and fills more of the frame with every fraction of a second.

[17.5-20.0] She hits the water. The frame goes to churning white, then punches through into the pool: turquoise, bubble-filled, muffled below the surface. She rises and breaks through the surface. The lens clears in streaks and the last thing in the frame is her two bare feet floating in bright turquoise water under a deep blue Toronto sky.

## END STATE

The rider is floating on her back in a lit turquoise rooftop pool in Toronto at blue-hour dusk. Her two bare feet break the surface in the lower third of the frame. Water still churns and foams from her entry. The bowed fisheye horizon shows rooftops, the deep blue sky and the thin cloud band, and far above and behind her the CN Tower she started from.
