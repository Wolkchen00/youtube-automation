## BEATS

[0.0-2.0] From the very first moment the rider is already walking briskly barefoot along the narrow glass walkway toward the slide, left foot, right foot, never stopping. Through the clear glass under her feet there is only empty air and the land far below. The open mouth of the slide grows with every step, its two neon saffron rims curving away and down toward the cloud, and the Taj Mahal waits far below and ahead beyond it. She never stands still and never hesitates.

[2.0-4.0] She reaches the mouth, sits straight down into it feet first and pushes off, and the slide takes her at once into the steep plunge. The Taj Mahal fills the centre of the frame, its great white onion dome rising over the tall arched gateway of the main hall, the four minarets standing at the corners of the marble platform. The slide drops past the gold finial on top of the dome close enough to see the white marble panels and the inlaid flowers of coloured stone rush up and past on the left, the long garden and the cypress trees far below. Below, the white sea of cloud rushes up toward her and the slide dives straight into it.

[4.0-6.0] The slide plunges into the cloud. The frame becomes an even, flat grey-white murk; only her two legs, both feet and the two neon saffron rims stay readable. The air turns heavy and steaming wet, the murk inside the cloud darkens to slate grey, and warm monsoon rain starts to stream down the lens.

[6.0-8.5] She drops out of the underside of the cloud into the same garden in a monsoon cloudburst. The Taj Mahal glows pale white against a black sky, lit hard white by lightning, and rain pours down in grey sheets. Behind it the Yamuna has burst its banks: a wide brown flood is sweeping down the river and pouring over the riverside walls into the garden.

[8.5-10.5] The flood water rises fast. A brown surge pours through the garden, swallowing the water channel and the cypress trees and climbing the steps of the marble platform, and lightning lights the white dome for an instant. Rain hammers the acrylic so hard that brown water runs over the clear floor under her feet, and uprooted trees roll past on the current below.

[10.5-13.0] The main flood wave comes roaring down the Yamuna and over the walls toward the slide from the right side of the frame, a churning wall of brown water and mud rising up the marble platform, with uprooted trees, broken boats and wooden debris tumbling inside it, its foaming crest lit white by lightning, growing every second and filling more and more of the frame.

[13.0-15.0] The slide runs straight into it, both legs and both feet still separately visible and the two neon saffron rims still burning. The flood wave smashes into the camera: brown water surges across the lens, the frame floods dark muddy brown under one last white flash, then dims to near black as the water closes over the lens.
