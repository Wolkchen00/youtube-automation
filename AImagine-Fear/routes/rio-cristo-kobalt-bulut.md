## OPENING STATE

The rider stands barefoot on the stone viewing terrace at the feet of Christ the Redeemer on Corcovado in Rio de Janeiro, at night. The weather is a warm night with cloud wrapping the peak in slow drifts. Her two bare legs and bare feet fill the bottom third of the frame, wet and shining on the pale mosaic stone. The floodlights set into the terrace throw her shadow upward. Above and behind her the statue rises, and its two outstretched arms end inside the cloud, cut off at the wrists by white. Beyond the terrace edge there is no ground at all: a soft cloud mass lies below her, and the rounded dome of Sugarloaf Mountain pushes up through it. Far ahead on the terrace, small in the distance, sits the open mouth of a fully transparent acrylic slide, its two upswept rims outlined by a continuous cobalt blue strip. It runs off the terrace edge and down into the cloud. There is no railing anywhere between her feet and the drop.

## BEATS

[0.0-2.0] The rider stands still at the terrace edge and the camera on her chest holds the drop dead centre. Her toes press into the wet mosaic. The transparent slide mouth waits ahead of her, its cobalt blue rims the only saturated colour in the frame. Below her feet there is no city, only the pale top of the cloud with Sugarloaf standing up out of it. At the top of the frame the statue's stone hand hangs half inside the cloud, going nowhere. She does not move yet. The stillness is the fear.

[2.0-4.0] She edges forward toward the mouth in short unsteady steps. The mouth grows continuously and never jumps in size: first a small outline, then a pair of upswept transparent rims taller than her shins, then a wide open throat. Behind it the cloud keeps its exact place in the frame and the slide is visible entering it far below, a cobalt line going into white. Her legs shake visibly.

[4.0-6.0] She sits and drops into the throat of the slide. The terrace leaves the frame entirely and does not come back. The two cobalt rims snap into hard converging perspective, running away from her hips toward the white surface below, and the solid clear floor between them is readable the whole way down. The first steep pitch takes her straight at the cloud and the top of the cloud rises to meet her feet.

[6.0-7.8] She goes into the cloud. White closes around the slide on both sides and Sugarloaf is gone. The clear floor under her legs stays transparent and the glow of Rio keeps reading through it, dimmer but never absent, a wide amber field moving under her feet. The floodlights on the peak behind her turn the cloud a lit blue-white for a moment, then fall away. The two cobalt rims burn at exactly the same brightness they had in open air, converging ahead into white with no end visible. Water beads crawl sideways across the lens. Her legs are the only hard edges in the frame.

[7.8-10.0] She comes out of the bottom of the cloud and Rio is suddenly close. Guanabara Bay lies directly beneath the clear floor, black and wide, with the lit crescent of the beach curving away on one side and the bare rounded rock of Sugarloaf standing up ahead of her. The slide bottoms out and a wall of white water bursts up around her hips and across the lens, lit cobalt from beneath by the rims, then clears in streaks. Speed becomes obvious: the rims are streaming past the frame edges.

[10.0-12.5] The slide banks hard into one long continuous curve carried on thin struts, already visible ahead before she reaches it. The whole bay rotates around her legs while her legs stay in the same place in the frame. Sugarloaf swings across one side of the frame and the black water fills the other. Spray flies outward along the wall of the turn. Coming out of it the horizon settles far lower and the peak she started from is a lit point above the cloud behind her, the statue on it now smaller than her own knee.

[12.5-15.0] Straight ahead and growing, a long cobalt-lit pool on a terrace deck at the foot of Sugarloaf waits with its far edge open to the bay. The final chute aims at it. She hits the water, the frame goes to churning white for a beat, then punches through into the pool: bright, bubble-filled, muffled under the surface. She rises and breaks through at the open edge, where the water runs over the lip and falls away into the dark.

## END STATE

The rider hangs at the open overflow edge of a cobalt-lit pool on a terrace deck at the foot of Sugarloaf Mountain in Rio de Janeiro, both forearms on the wet lip, water pouring over it past her and dropping away into the black bay below. Her two bare feet trail behind her in the pool, breaking the surface in the lower third of the frame. The bowed fisheye horizon holds the lit beachfront and, far above and behind, the cloud she came down through with the floodlit head and shoulders of Christ the Redeemer standing clear of the top of it.
