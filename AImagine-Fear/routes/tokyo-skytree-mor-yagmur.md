She does not move yet. The stillness is the fear.

[2.0-4.0] She edges forward toward the mouth in short unsteady steps. [4.0-6.0] She sits and drops into the throat of the slide. The deck leaves the frame entirely and does not come back. [6.0-8.0] The slide bottoms out and a wall of white water bursts up around her hips and across the lens. [10.0-11.5] She holds that curve and the city rolls all the way around the frame, once, continuously, never cutting and never jumping. Spray flies outward along the wall of the turn. [11.5-13.0] The slide drops into a canyon between two dark towers. [13.0-15.0] She hits the water. The frame goes to churning white for a beat, then punches through into the pool: bright, bubble-filled, muffled under the surface. She rises and breaks through.
