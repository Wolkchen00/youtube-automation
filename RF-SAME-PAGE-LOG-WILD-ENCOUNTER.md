## Direct answers

**Q1.** No: `music: false` guarantees only that the post-production Suno/music branch is skipped; `master_lufs: -14` independently invokes `master_audio` and verifies ±1 LU plus ≤−1 dBTP. Omni can still generate music in its native soundtrack. On this series, the separately enabled `qc.native_audio_review` detects that case and fails/regenerates the shot.

Other relevant branches:

- `_reserve_plan_music` reads `bible.music`.
- `_post_process` reads `bible.music`, sets `music_ok`, and rejects when `required_music` is true but no music was produced.
- `produce_episode` reads `required_layers`; `"music"` would make `music:false` fail only after video generation, while `"native_audio"` activates the final audio gate.
- `preflight` validates `required_layers` names but does not reject the contradictory combination `music:false` plus required `"music"`.
- `auto_replenish.music_prompt` independently controls whether future plans request a `music` field.
- `audio_smooth`, `chain_frames`, durable artifacts, and state-machine v2 do not introduce a music bed.

**Q2.** No. The critic receives `kwargs["prompt"]` for Omni and `rv["prompt"]` for other engines: the composed generation prompt beginning with `art_style`, followed by the raw `SHOT N` paragraph and reference bindings. The proposed note can refer to “the paragraph beginning `SHOT N,`”, but cannot truthfully call it the opening paragraph.

`native_audio_review` fails on either `unwanted_speech=true` or `unwanted_music=true`. `has_foley=false` is only counted and does not fail. Therefore music generated by Omni itself can already be flagged and regenerated without new code.

**Q3.** No tek-obje-only gate is activated merely by adding those fields:

- `format_plan_errors` remains guarded by `format_version == "tek-obje-4x6"`.
- `require_object_match` alone controls object-reference download and its hard QC gate.
- ROCK B `anomaly_match` is actually format-agnostic: `object_card.anomaly_descriptor` requests the metric, while `qc.enforce.anomaly_match` activates the gate. The current plan already supplies that descriptor.
- Preflight generically resolves Omni references and checks the seven-unit limit; this is not a tek-obje gate.
- `prop_ref_urls` changes both durable plan and reference fingerprints. `creature_anchor` would change the full-plan fingerprint but is otherwise unknown to current fingerprint logic.

The local `register_character` wrapper performs no subject-type validation, but neither the code nor Kie’s documentation demonstrates non-human support; Kie’s example is human. Its endpoint response provides no credit usage. Kie states that generation services require credits, so I would treat registration as paid until measured, but its exact cost remains unknown. [Kie character endpoint](https://docs.kie.ai/market/gemini-omni-character), [Kie account-credit guidance](https://docs.kie.ai/cn/common-api/get-account-credits).

**Q4.** No production process bulk-validates every `plans/part*.json`. Produce and preflight validate only the supplied plan; calibration scans plans for records but does not call this validator. Part02–04 are already incompatible with the current plato-3x8 configuration. Part05 currently matches the old `shot_plan` and would become incompatible after Rock 3, but nothing breaks immediately unless it is explicitly reproduced or preflighted.

**Q5.** Use the already-supported image-only path: generate one creature image into `plan.prop_ref_urls`, generate/reuse the environment’s `ref_image_url`, and combine those with episode-scoped chain frames. This removes non-human registration, duplicate subject conditioning, unknown registration cost, and the custom `creature_anchor` structure.
